# Digital Twins & Gazebo

This chapter covers digital twin concepts and Gazebo simulation.

## What are Digital Twins?

A digital twin is a virtual representation of a physical object or system. In robotics, a digital twin can simulate the robot's physical behavior, its environment, and its interactions, allowing for testing and development in a safe, virtual space.

## Gazebo for Physics Simulation

Gazebo is a powerful open-source 3D robot simulator. It provides the ability to accurately and efficiently simulate populations of robots in complex indoor and outdoor environments. Gazebo offers:
-   **Physics Engine**: High-fidelity physics engines for realistic interactions.
-   **Sensors**: Simulation of a wide range of sensors.
-   **Environments**: The ability to create complex 3D environments.
